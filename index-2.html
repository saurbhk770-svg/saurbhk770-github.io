❓ Student Help Desk

Common questions, answered quickly

Where can I submit an examination grievance?

Through the AKTU Online Student Grievance Redressal Portal. Keep the generated Grievance ID for tracking.

What can the grievance portal be used for?

Examination results, result correction/re-evaluation, marks update/correction, marksheet issues, degree issues and other examination-related grievances.

When did Odd Semester classes start?

Classes for the Odd Semester commenced from 01 August 2026.

Where is AIMT located?

The main institute is on Maurawan Road, Mohanlalganj, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh – 226301.
